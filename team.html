Meet the Team

Leadership that blends nursing, management, and hospitality.

Peaceful Harmony is owner-operated, with clinical oversight led by Aster Birri, RN, and administration and operations led by Tesfa Bogale.

Home/Team
Meet the Owners

Two complementary roles. One shared home.

Aster and Tesfa combine professional experience with a personal commitment to making residents feel safe, known, welcomed, and at home.

Co-Owner • RN
AB
Registered Nurse • Clinical Oversight

Aster Birri, RN

Aster is the heart of the care at Peaceful Harmony. She has more than 20 years of nursing experience, caring for people who need anything from everyday assistance to complex medical care.

She started Peaceful Harmony because she wanted to bring that experience into a smaller, more personal setting where she could truly know each resident and understand their individual needs. As the home's RN and owner, Aster oversees resident care, medications, staff training, care coordination, and the overall safety and quality of the home.

Her goal: for every person who comes into the home to feel cared for, respected, and at peace.

Co-Owner
TB
Administrator • Management & Operations

Tesfa Bogale

Tesfa manages the day-to-day business side of Peaceful Harmony, including staffing, finances, payroll, and operations. He also spends time with residents, helps with activities, cooks, plays music, and brings plenty of humor into the home.

With a background in technology and management, Tesfa helps keep the home organized and running smoothly while Aster leads the clinical care.

His goal is simple: residents should feel at home.

Our Care Team

Caregivers supported by clear expectations and RN oversight.

Individual caregiver identities are kept private online. The care team supports residents with daily living, mobility, meals, personal care, companionship, housekeeping, and other assigned needs under Peaceful Harmony’s care plans and leadership.

Consistency

A smaller setting allows residents and caregivers to become familiar with routines, preferences, and day-to-day needs.

Communication

Aster oversees staff training and care coordination, while families receive updates as needed by phone and in person.

Respect

Caregivers are expected to provide patient, dependable, person-centered support while maintaining a safe, clean, welcoming home.

How We Work Together

Clinical leadership meets thoughtful operations.

Aster leads nursing oversight, care coordination, staff training, medications, supplies, and resident care needs.

Tesfa leads administration, staffing, payroll, finances, operations, and hospitality while remaining involved in daily resident life.

Together, they aim to provide the professionalism families expect with the warmth and flexibility of a small home.
