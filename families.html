How to Get Started

A simple path from first conversation to move-in.

01

Talk With Us

Tell us about the person’s daily routine, mobility, medications, personal-care needs, health needs, preferences, and what your family is looking for.

02

Schedule a Tour

Tours are by appointment. Visit the home, see the living spaces, ask questions, and get a feel for whether the environment is right.

03

Assessment & Next Steps

Aster reviews care needs to determine whether Peaceful Harmony can safely meet them. If it is a good fit, the family is guided through the next steps for admission and move-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions families often ask.

An adult foster home is a residential care setting where adults who need support can receive individualized assistance in a smaller, home-like environment. Peaceful Harmony is a Level 3 adult foster home in Beaverton.

The home serves up to five residents. Resident bedrooms are private rather than shared.

Care needs are reviewed before admission. The assessment looks at daily assistance, mobility, medications, health needs, behavior, routines, equipment, and other relevant information so the home can determine whether the person can be supported safely.

Yes. Tours are appointment-only so there is dedicated time to show you the home, answer questions, and learn about the person you are seeking care for.

Yes. Meals are cooked in the home. Individual dietary needs, preferences, and feeding assistance can be discussed as part of the resident’s care planning.

Medication management and oversight are coordinated as part of the resident’s care plan under Aster Birri, RN. Specific medication needs are reviewed during assessment and care planning.

Communication happens as needed by phone and in person. Families can speak directly with the home about important updates, questions, and changing needs.

Visiting is flexible. Peaceful Harmony works with individual families to coordinate schedules in a way that supports the resident and the household.

Every resident is different. Everyday options include television, visitors, card games, board games, gentle exercises, conversation, and quiet time based on what the resident enjoys.

Peaceful Harmony is a smoke-free, pet-free home. Individuals with high behavioral-support needs may not be appropriate for the setting. Final admission decisions are based on individual assessment and whether needs can be safely met.

Room availability can change. Contact Peaceful Harmony for the most current information about openings.

Before Move-In

We’ll help your family prepare.

Before admission, we review what to bring, medication information, personal belongings, equipment, documents, and any other resident-specific needs so the family knows what to expect.
