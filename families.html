Before You Choose a Home

The important questions deserve clear answers.

We encourage families to verify licensing, discuss the person’s specific care needs, understand current availability, and ask about costs before making a decision.

1. Verify Licensing

Peaceful Harmony is an Oregon-licensed Adult Foster Home. The official state provider record lists the home as open with 5 licensed beds.

View our official Oregon provider record ↗

2. Confirm Care Fit

Every prospective resident is assessed before admission. Tell us about mobility and transfer needs, medications, memory or behavioral needs, nighttime support, dietary needs, equipment, and other important routines so we can determine whether the home can safely meet those needs.

3. Check Availability

Peaceful Harmony has 5 licensed resident beds and private resident rooms. Openings can change, so contact us for current room availability or waitlist information.

4. Ask About Costs

Rates depend on the resident’s assessed care needs and current arrangements. Contact us for current private-pay rates and what is included. Oregon’s state listing identifies the home as accepting Medicaid; individual eligibility and payment arrangements should be discussed directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions families often ask.

An adult foster home is a residential care setting where adults who need support can receive individualized assistance in a smaller, home-like environment. Peaceful Harmony is a Level 3 adult foster home in Beaverton.

The home serves up to five residents. Resident bedrooms are private rather than shared.

Care needs are reviewed before admission. The assessment looks at daily assistance, mobility, medications, health needs, behavior, routines, equipment, and other relevant information so the home can determine whether the person can be supported safely.

Yes. Tours are appointment-only so there is dedicated time to show you the home, answer questions, and learn about the person you are seeking care for.

Yes. Meals are cooked in the home. Individual dietary needs, preferences, and feeding assistance can be discussed as part of the resident’s care planning.

Medication management and oversight are coordinated as part of the resident’s care plan under RN oversight. Specific medication needs are reviewed during assessment and care planning.

Communication happens as needed by phone and in person. Families can speak directly with the home about important updates, questions, and changing needs.

Visiting is flexible. Peaceful Harmony works with individual families to coordinate schedules in a way that supports the resident and the household.

Every resident is different. Everyday options include television, visitors, card games, board games, gentle exercises, conversation, and quiet time based on what the resident enjoys.

Peaceful Harmony is a smoke-free, pet-free home. Individuals with high behavioral-support needs may not be appropriate for the setting. Final admission decisions are based on individual assessment and whether needs can be safely met.

The Oregon Department of Human Services provider listing identifies Peaceful Harmony as accepting Medicaid. Coverage and payment arrangements depend on the individual situation, so please contact us to discuss details.
