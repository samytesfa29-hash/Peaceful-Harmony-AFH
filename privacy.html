Your information should be handled with care.

This policy explains what information Peaceful Harmony may receive through this website, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Privacy Policy

Clear, simple information about your privacy.

Effective August 10, 2026. This policy applies to the Peaceful Harmony Adult Foster Home website, including tour/contact inquiries and caregiver applications.

Information you provide

When you contact us or request a tour, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, who you are seeking care for, and a message about your inquiry.

When you apply for a caregiver position, you may provide contact information, work availability, experience, qualifications, training information, work history, and other information you choose to include in your application.

How we use it

We use information submitted through the website to respond to inquiries, discuss tours and possible admission, communicate with families, review caregiver applications, arrange interviews, and manage related follow-up.

We do not use website form information for unrelated marketing unless you separately agree to receive it.

Website service providers

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Website forms are processed through Formspree so submissions can be delivered to Peaceful Harmony. The Contact page also includes an embedded Google Map, and the site loads web fonts from Google.

Those third-party services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy practices when their services are used.

Information we do not want through forms

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, driver's-license numbers, bank or payment information, copies of identification, or detailed medical records through the public website forms.

The tour/contact form is intended for general inquiries and initial care discussions, not urgent medical communication.

Sharing and selling information

Peaceful Harmony does not sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or process submissions, and when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect safety or legal rights.

How long information is kept

We keep website inquiries and job-application information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, for follow-up, recordkeeping, or as required by applicable law. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or securely disposed of.

Security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to submitted information and use reputable service providers. No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information through public website forms.

Your choices

You may contact Peaceful Harmony to ask about personal information you previously submitted through the website, request a correction, or request deletion when appropriate. Some information may need to be retained for legal, operational, or recordkeeping reasons.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking information about adult foster care or employment. We do not knowingly use the website to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, forms, service providers, or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
