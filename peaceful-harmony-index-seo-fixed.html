Our Name, Our Purpose

Peace for the individual. Harmony in the home.

Peaceful Harmony was created from a simple idea: open a home to people who may begin as strangers, then build an environment where each person can experience peace, belonging, dignity, and harmony together.

Peaceful Harmony is an Oregon-licensed Level 3 Adult Foster Home. Aster’s nursing experience and Tesfa’s management, hospitality, and day-to-day operational support come together in a small residential setting focused on individualized care.

Licensed & Transparent

Families should be able to confirm important information for themselves. Peaceful Harmony is listed by the Oregon Department of Human Services as an open Adult Foster Home provider with 5 licensed beds. The state listing also identifies the home as accepting Medicaid.
