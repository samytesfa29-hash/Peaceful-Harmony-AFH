A SMALLER, MORE PERSONAL SETTING

A place designed to feel like home.

With a maximum of five residents, Peaceful Harmony offers a quieter residential environment where care can be personalized around each person’s needs, routines, preferences, and comfort.

01

Private Rooms

Each available room is private. Peaceful Harmony does not offer shared resident rooms.

02

RN-Managed Care

Care is supported by experienced Registered Nurse oversight, care coordination, staff training, and individualized planning.

03

Home-Cooked Meals

Meals are prepared in the home, helping create the familiar rhythm and comfort of everyday home life.

04

Accessible Home

The home includes accessibility features such as wheelchair access, ramps, grab bars, and accessible bathing areas.
