Our Home

A smaller setting with a more personal feel.

A private-room adult foster home designed around comfort, accessibility, safety, and familiar daily routines.

Home/Our Home
Home at a Glance

Professional support without losing the feeling of home.

Peaceful Harmony serves up to five residents in a residential environment where individual needs can receive close attention.

5

Small Residential Setting

Serving up to five residents helps the home stay personal, familiar, and attentive.

Private Resident Rooms

Resident rooms are private rather than shared, supporting comfort, privacy, and personal space.

Accessible by Design

Wheelchair-friendly spaces, ramps, grab bars, accessible bedrooms and bathrooms, and accessible bathing features support safer daily living.

Everyday Life

Simple routines. Familiar comforts. Room to be yourself.

01

Home-Cooked Meals

Meals are prepared in the home, with individual needs and preferences discussed as part of the resident’s care.

02

Activities & Companionship

Residents may enjoy television, visitors, card games, board games, gentle exercises, conversation, and quiet time according to their preferences.

03

Flexible Family Visits

Visiting is handled flexibly with individual families so schedules can be coordinated in a way that works for the resident and household.

04

Cleanliness, Safety & Flexibility

These are three priorities central to the way Peaceful Harmony is operated every day.

Our Philosophy

The home should adapt to the resident—not the other way around.

01

Dignity

Residents are adults with preferences, routines, histories, and choices that deserve respect.

02

Individualization

Support is shaped around assessed needs and may change as the resident’s condition or routine changes.

03

Belonging

The goal is for residents to experience the familiarity and warmth of home, not the feeling of a large institution.
