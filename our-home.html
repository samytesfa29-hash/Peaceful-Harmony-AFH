What Is an Adult Foster Home?

A home-based care setting for adults who need support with daily life.

This section is intentionally written in plain language for families. Later, you can add Oregon-specific licensing language or links if you want.

01

Home-Like Environment

Residents live in a residential setting designed to feel more personal and familiar.

02

Daily Assistance

Care can include personal support, meals, mobility assistance, medication support, and other services based on assessed needs.

03

Smaller Setting

A smaller household environment may allow caregivers to know residents and routines more closely.

04

Family Connection

Families can communicate directly with the people providing day-to-day support.
